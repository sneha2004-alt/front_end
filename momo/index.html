How Momos Are Made The Dumpoo Way

Every pretzel starts with a slow-mixed dough, shaped by hand, rested until tender, and baked until the crust turns glossy and golden. It is the kind of everyday craft that makes a simple snack feel like a small celebration. Every momo begins with a thin wrapper, a fresh filling, and careful folds that seal in the flavor. We steam each batch until tender, finish select orders with a crisp fry or tandoori toss, and serve everything with chutney that brings the heat.
