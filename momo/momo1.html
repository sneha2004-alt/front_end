Ozzie & the team

At Ozzie's Pretzels, we keep things simple: red ingredients, small batches, and handmade care in every step. We're a woman-owned, Denver-based bakery that started in a tiny home kitchen—and so we'd know every pretzel the same way. We use no preservatives, no shortcuts, just soft, warm, authentic comfort delivered to your door. When you order from us, you're not just getting a snack—you're supporting a local small batch bakery with a big heart.

How It's Made

The Ozzie's Way

Every Ozzie's pretzel start with a simple idea: handmade. make it with care, and make it feel like home. We're not a factory—we're a small team in Denver doing what we love. From the scenic early mornings, kneading dough, the smell of butter and sea salt in the air, and hand shaping every twist with purpose. It's slow work... and that's the point.
